Hollowmere

The Count recommended · optional

Nothing here is required. Walk anywhere, enter anything, leave however you can find. This is only one way through.

  1. Five flames still burn in the houses of the Oath: Pell, Hendry, Marsh, Orme, Tolliver. Read the nameplates over the doors.
  2. Carry each flame out. Candles on the upper floors burn higher than the ones below.
  3. The gate opens when all five are in your hands. Go back south.

Side pursuits: find all the pages, learn the rules of the Count, find the places the village hides. There may be another way out.

Find the five candles. The gate stays shut until all of them are in your hands.

A game of looking

Hollowmere

Five candles. One gate. Do not look away.

You arrived after dusk, and the gate closed behind you. That makes you uncounted, and the uncounted are what the Count takes.

What you should know

Five flames still burn in the houses of the Oath. Carry them back to the gate. Something walks the lanes. It only moves when you are not looking at it, and walls do not stop it. Stare too long and it comes for you. There are cupboards. There may be other ways out.
