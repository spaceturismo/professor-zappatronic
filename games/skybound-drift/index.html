How to play

Walk
Hold to move. Your speed keeps building the longer you hold it. Let go to slow down.
Look
Turn and look up or down.
Jump
The small button above Walk. Also jumps you off ladders and out of water.
Ladders
Walk into a yellow ladder to climb. Climbing speeds up too.
Item
Uses whatever you picked in the bag. Hidden items glow with a tall beam of light.
Grappling hook
Hold the Item button to fire a web line and swing. Hold Jump at the same time to reel yourself straight to the target instead; let go of Jump to go back to swinging. Steer with Walk, push forward to shorten the line, let go of the item to fling.
Jetpack and Dash
Hold the Jetpack to fly up until the fuel ring empties; it refuels on the ground. Dash bursts you the way you look.
Checkpoints
Touch a stone pad with a light beam to light it. Lit checkpoints let you fast travel from the map, and Back to checkpoint takes you to the last one you touched.
Stardust
48 small glowing stars hide on rooftops, mesas, islands, peaks and even underwater.
Pause
The pause button can be moved like the other controls in Settings, Rearrange.
The bubble
Stay inside. Ten seconds outside and something comes for you.
Apocalypse mode
Start it from the title screen or the pause menu. Every enemy glows through walls in its own colour (green zombies, orange brutes, purple aliens, red drones, pink for The Void), and arrows at the screen edge point to ones behind you. The Item button fires your weapon and the bag switches weapons. Survive waves: a Zombie Outbreak, a Robot Uprising, an Alien Invasion, a Meteor Storm, then The Void itself. Each wave cycle gets harder. New weapons unlock as you survive. Zombies climb walls, drones fly and shoot, aliens leap, meteors can be shot out of the sky. Health comes back if you avoid damage for a few seconds, and green crosses heal you.

Skybound Drift

A city under floating islands, a lantern lake, red mesas, a pine forest, windmill fields and snowy peaks, all inside a bubble you shouldn't leave.

Best played with your phone sideways.

Explore at your own pace, or grab a weapon and survive waves of zombies, robots, aliens, meteors and The Void.
