Hollow Pines

The last caretaker of the Hollow Pines house stopped answering his radio three weeks ago. Tonight the job is yours: sit at the desk from midnight until 6 AM and watch the six cameras.

  • Memorise each room. When something changes, an object moved or missing, something new, a figure, writing on the wall, the lights, a possessed object, file a report naming the room and the kind of anomaly.
  • Five unreported anomalies at once and they take you. Every one left standing drains your sanity.
  • False reports cost sanity. At low sanity you will start seeing things that aren't there.
  • The EMF reader twitches near anomalies, but it lies. The spirit box whispers where they are, but it lies too. Sage cleanses one room completely; you get one more at 3 AM.

Keys: 1–6 or ←/→ switch cameras, R reports. Sound on recommended. The first minute is quiet: learn the rooms.
