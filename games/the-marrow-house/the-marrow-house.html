THE MARROW HOUSERecovered tape, Hollow Pines County, 1994

The Marrow family recorded everything. Then they stopped answering the door. You are inside their house now, and the front door has locked behind you.

Find the family's seven VHS tapes. They are spread across three floors. When you have all seven, the front door opens.

You are not alone in the house. The county's emergency broadcasts will cut in to tell you the rules for each thing you meet. Listen to them. You can be caught three times.
