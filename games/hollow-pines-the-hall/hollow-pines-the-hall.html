Hollow PinesThe upstairs hall

You woke up in the upstairs hall of a house you don't remember. Every door brings you back to the start of the same hall. A brass plaque counts how far you've got.

Learn the hall. If anything is different (something you see, something you hear, something that happens as you walk), turn around and go back through the door behind you. If nothing is different, walk on through the far door. A wrong choice sends the count back to zero. Reach Hall 8 to escape.

Some of what lives here will come for you if you pretend not to notice it.
